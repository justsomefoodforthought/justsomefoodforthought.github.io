I like to think I have experienced every kind of emotion the universe has to offer, but then life throws me a curveball. 

I just watched Hasan Minaj’s new movie “Best of the Best,” and it made me feel some type of way. 

There are two things this film triggered: heritage erasure and an inferiority complex. 

I feel like when I was younger. I wanted nothing to do with being Indian (except the food that I will claim.) From the itchy half saris to the hours on end poojas, I disliked everything in between. 

But, as I’ve gotten older thought things have changed. I didn’t mind the lehenga that weighed a ton or the earring post that made my ears bleed. Somewhere along the way I started to enjoy it. 

In college, when I finally had fellow brown peers actually embracing their culture by going to things like Garba or frat parties that exclusively played Bollywood songs it felt weird, but comforting, like finally someone gets it. 

Seeing this on my Netflix, made me realize this is how it should be. I shoudln’t have had to be embarrassed about the music I lisented to or the random traditions I had. And idk bro. How the helly did we get a movie about a red panda before brown people? Like let’s lock in humanity. (No hate to Turning Red that was a fantabulous movie). 

Moving on, time to talk about why this film made me think about my inferiority complex. Maya in the film is overshadowed by Anj(ali) her whole life. Even though this may be a far fetched connection. This is how I have felt about my friends and I my whole life. No matter what chapter I’m in and no matter what I have going on for me in my life, it always feels like the people around me, my friends are always on a step one step higher. 

Like in middle school I was good enough for the dance team but not enough for officer. In high school I was good enough for the advanced classes but not smart enough to be number 1. It’s not one specific person it just feel like everyone around me is just better than me. And yea blah blah blah I know everyone has their “thing.” I just feel like no matter what I do, I keep playing supporting character in others life, like a point of comparison. 

Anyways, thank you Hasan for making me think which is low-key rare these days. 

Film Rating: 10.10 would recommend.
